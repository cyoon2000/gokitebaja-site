Date : Sat, Nov 26th, 2016 <br/>
Price : 135 USD*
</h4>
<p>The proceeds from the event go towards the La Ventana classic cause. We help put kids to school</p>
<h4> Message from Marie at 
<a href="https://gokitebaja.com/elevation-kiteboarding/"> Elevation Kiteboarding </a> </h4>
<p>Women's day on the island is a super fun outing for the ladies. I started doing this a couple years ago with a friend of mine, Laura Maher, a slingshot rider.</p>
<p>We take all the ladies that want to join us to <b>Cerralvo island</b> in the morning before the wind picks up. Then we have a nice little relaxing yoga session on the beach followed by some kite talk.</p>
<p>Then we all get ready to make the <b>11 mile crossing back to La Ventana </b> for happy hour.</p>
<p>This season we will be going to the island twice. This next november 26th and also one day in January during the classics.</p>
