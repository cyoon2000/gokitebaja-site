<p>Each camp is $450 dollars for three days of kitesurfing lessons and yoga. The day will start with Yoga at 9-10 am. We will have the option of doing a Vinyasa Power Class or a Yin Yoga Class. When the wind starts we will do 2-3 hours of kite lessons each day depending on the wind conditions. Price includes a welcome dinner for everyone and drink on the last day. If you have children bring them they can play in the kite kids daycare during the lessons. There must be a minimum of three people per camp or the camp is subject to cancellation.<br>
EMAIL to book your trip <a href="mailto:nicole-eden@hotmail.com">nicole-eden@hotmail.com</a></p>
<h4>The Team</h4>
We are an IKO Certified Kite School. Which means everyone of our instructors have been trained with the highest safety standards and newest techniques to help you learn faster. We focus on making you an independent kiteboarder. That includes making sure you are comfortable setting up your equipment, launching and landing on your own, getting in and out of the water safely in addition to helping you to learn to ride~~We offer lessons in English, Spanish, and French. Our team is an all female team that offers a great environment to be around and we all teaches because they love it ! We use brand new Cabrinha kites, headzone radio helmuts, ion harness's and wetsuits. We offer jet ski or ground lessons depending on your needs. 
<p>Jet ski assisted lessons are the fastest easiest way to learn to kiteboard. We suggest you book your jet ski lesson when you are at the level of learning to ride the board. The jet ski is also a great tool for kiters who want advanced lessons on the surfboard, twin tip or on the foil. Please note that prices in Canada include the jet ski for the board lesson this is an optional feature in our Mexican location</p>
