<h4> Welcome Cascade Kiteboarding in Baja </h4>
Cascade Kiteboarding brings its professional and personalized lesson format and progressive learning tools to Baja! 2-way Bluetooth helmets for instant feedback and instruction. Quads and boats to follow you, and only new 2016 and 2017 gear from North Kiteboarding and Liquid Force! You can pump up and launch just steps from your casita, or even better from the Palapas Ventana hot tub and bar.
