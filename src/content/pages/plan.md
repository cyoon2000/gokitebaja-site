<h4 id="whenisbesttimetokite">When is the best time to kite?</h4>
<p><b>November to March.</b><br>
Ventana Bay is famous for consistent north winds (20-25kts) that blow during winter. A combination of El Nortes (northerly fronts) and local thermal winds make it possible to kite almost every day during the winter season. The steady side-shore winds, smooth medium-sized swells, wide sandy beaches, and huge area to kite make Ventana Bay perfect for people at all experience levels.</p>

<h5 id="shuttle">Shuttle</h5>
<ul>
<li>
<p>There is no regular scheduled shuttle running, but you can arrange shuttle service to get to La Ventana. (one way or round trip)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Price is fixed per trip, divided by number of passengers.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The driver would make a stop at Los Barriles for dinner (You can ask the driver to go to a restaurant of your choice in Los Barriles, or ask the driver to pick any). And you may be able to do some grocery shopping here if your driver does not mind waiting. (There are smaller grocery stores in La Ventana.)</p>
</li>
</ul>

<h4 id="faq">FAQ</h4>
<h5 id="doineedtobringwetsuit">Do I need to bring wetsuit?</h5>
<p>Depends on if you get easily get cold or not. The water temps are in the 70sF. Shorty is good for Spring/Fall. Most of people wear 3/2mm full wetsuit for mid winter.</p>
<h5 id="baggagefeeforkiteboard">Baggage fee for Kiteboard</h5>
<p>I noticed recently major Airlines such as American Air and United Air added a section specific for <B>kiteboard</B> or <B>kitesurfing Equipment</B> in Baggage Fee Information.<br>
I have summarized the terms and the link below for the major airlines flying into SJD. But please note that I do not guarantee the following information is 100% correct or won't change.</p>
<p>Virgin America and Alaskan Air still seems to remain as surf-board friendly airlines as of Jan 2016.($50/$75 vs $150/$200 by American and United) If you bring usual Twin tip kiteboard which is within limit, it seems that you can check it in as standard 1st checked bag with the airlines below.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.alaskaair.com/content/travel-info/baggage/special-baggage/traveling-with-sporting-equipment.aspx">Alaskan Air Baggage Info</a></p>
<ul>
<li>Standard 1st checked-bag : $25</li>
<li>Surfboard type : $75</li>
</ul>
<p><a href="https://www.americanairlines.it/i18n/travel-information/baggage/sports-items.jsp?locale=en_HK">American Air Baggage Info</a></p>
<ul>
<li>Standard 1st checked-bag : $25</li>
<li>Oversize or Surfboard type : $150</li>
<li>Oversize = Exceeding 157cm/62in (length + height + width)</li>
</ul>
<p><a href="https://www.united.com/web/en-US/content/travel/baggage/sports.aspx">United Air Baggage Info</a></p>
<ul>
<li>Standard 1st checked-bag : $25</li>
<li>Oversize or Surfboard type : $150 USD/CAD within US and Canada, $200 USD/CAD outside of the U.S. and Canada</li>
<li>Oversize = Exceeding 50 pounds (23 kg) or is greater than 62 (158 cm) total linear inches (L + W + H)</li>
</ul>
<p><a href="https://www.virginamerica.com/cms/vx-fees#/baggage">Virgin America Baggage Info</a></p>
<ul>
<li>Standard 1st checked-bag : $25</li>
<li>Surfboard type : $50</li>
<li>Oversize = Exceeding 62 linear inches</li>
</ul>
